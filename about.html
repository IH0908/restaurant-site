About Urban Bistro

Urban Bistro opened its doors in 2022 when a small team of friends decided the neighborhood needed a place that felt like a living room, but tasted like a downtown food festival.

Our kitchen focuses on bold flavors, seasonal ingredients, and a balance between comfort food and lighter, plant-forward dishes.

From the open kitchen to the playlist, everything is designed to make you feel welcome, whether you are grabbing a quick lunch, studying with coffee, or celebrating a late-night milestone.

We are proud to work with local farms and roasters, and we rotate specials weekly so there is always something new to discover.
